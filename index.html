About My Hometown

Favourite Photo of My Hometown

Ramechhap is a hill district in Bagmati Province, Nepal, with Manthali as its headquarters. It covers about 1,546 sq km and had around 202,646 people in the 2011 census. The name comes from the Nepali words "Ram" (a name) and "chhap" (mark or place). The district is also called Wallo Kirat Ramechhap. Its people include Magar, Sunuwar, Tamang, Newar, and Hayu communities, along with Nepali speakers. Ramechhap is known for Thulo Sailung, a hilltop famous for sunrise and sunset views, and the Numbur Cheese Circuit trek, which passes Nepal's first cheese factory and Thodung Monastery. Terraced farms, green hills, and the Likhu Khola valley make it a quiet and beautiful place.
